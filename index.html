Petrol Pump ERP

Software that runs a fuel station end to end: nozzle readings, tank stock, shifts and reconciliation, credit customers, procurement, payroll and an audit log. Six logins, from Owner down to Pump Attendant, each seeing only what they should.

Money records are never deleted. A wrong entry gets voided or reversed, and the reason stays on file.

Oil Spill Attribution

A pipeline that finds oil spills in SAR satellite radar images, then hands each detection to drift modelling and AIS ship-track matching to point at the vessel most likely responsible.

I moved training from Colab to my own GPU after re-downloading the dataset every session made iteration crawl. Every tuning run is logged against the best checkpoint, so I can tell what actually helped.

  • Semantic segmentation
  • SAR imagery
  • Drift modelling
  • AIS data
